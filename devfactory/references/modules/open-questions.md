# Open questions

A core module of the [card standard](../card-standard.md).

## Holds

Present only when something is open.

## Rules

Each question is stated in full so it stands on its own, names what it affects,
and gives the options where they are known. The question carries the
dependency: the parts it affects do not guess an answer, and, apart from a
dependent criterion's provisional mark, they do not repeat that they depend on
it. An open question does not record who it is waiting on or whether it has been
asked.
