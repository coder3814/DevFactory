# Summary

A core module of the [card standard](../card-standard.md).

## Holds

Two short sentences condensing what the card says in full below.

## Rules

The first states the outcome — what the system should do once the change is in
and what that produces — and names the requester where they are known and
clear. The second says what prompts the change: how things stand today. Detail
belongs to the sections below, and repeating a point from them is expected; that
is what a summary is. It uses wanted-state language, prescribes no method, and
never carries status.
