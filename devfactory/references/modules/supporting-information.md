# Supporting information

A core module of the [card standard](../card-standard.md).

## Holds

Present only when whoever does the work would otherwise miss or get wrong
something the earlier sections do not say: a rule or constraint the result must
respect, a decision already taken (stated as what was decided), or a definition
of a term the card relies on.

## Rules

Every item states what is true or what must hold, never what to do.
